IN DEPTH EXPLANATION

  1. Investment Strategy

    • We will be buying middle to low class vehicles. Most of the users on Turo are looking for a cheaper option than companies like Enterpries or Hertz. We will purchase our vehicles in the $8000-$12000, which will give us a better return on investment.
    • We will only be holding vehicles for 12-18 months, depending on the return that we have. This will minimize the risk of larger repairs on the car, and the car will hold value for resale.
    • Each car at the price range we have will be making relatively $500 a month after expenses, giving near $6000 return in a year.

  2. Potential Upside

    • Like said above, the average car will make around $500 a month after expenses. Data shows that many cars can earn up to $1000 a month, giving that car a 120% return.
    • As the new presidency increases Tarriffs, new car prices will increase, which in turn usally increases used car prices. During the time we own the car, if car prices increase there will be less loss every time we exchange cars.

  3. Risks

    • As cars are machines, many times they will break down. We mean to minimize this risk by holding each car for less time, but major repairs are bound to happen. If a car breaks down to the point of not being rentable, we will looke to fix it. If the repairs are more than expected, we will sell the car for parts and cut our losses.
    • Another risk is if the cars don't sell at the price we listed, we can lose money than expected when we exit.
